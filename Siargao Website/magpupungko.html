Why Visit?

Magpupungko Rock Pools are a natural wonder, forming crystal-clear basins at low tide. It’s a unique swimming experience surrounded by rugged rock formations.

“Swimming here feels surreal. We could only see this pool at low tides.” – Foreign Visitor
