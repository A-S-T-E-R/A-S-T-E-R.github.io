Top Attractions

Alegria Beach

A pristine stretch of white sand and turquoise waters in northern Siargao.

Cloud 9 Surfing Spot

World-famous waves that attract surfers from across the globe.

Doot Beach

A quiet beach surrounded by coconut trees, perfect for relaxation.

Island Hopping

Explore Daku, Guyam, and Naked Island for pristine beaches and crystal waters.

Natural tidal pools that reveal themselves during low tide.

Pacifico Beach

Known for its long stretch of sand and powerful surf breaks.

Pamomoan Beach

A hidden gem with calm waters and a peaceful vibe.

Secret Spot Beach

A secluded paradise offering tranquility away from the crowds.

Sugba Lagoon

A crystal-clear lagoon perfect for kayaking, paddleboarding, and relaxation.
